# Support

Need help with StratosToDo or the StratosToDo MCP connector?

- **Email**: munib.urehmann@gmail.com
- **Response time**: we aim to respond within 3 business days.

## Common issues

**The connector won't connect in Claude**
Make sure you're signed in to your StratosToDo account and try reconnecting
from Claude's Connectors settings.

**Claude says a request failed or something went wrong**
Try again in a moment — if it persists, email us with what you asked Claude
to do and roughly when it happened.

**I want my data deleted**
Email us from the address associated with your account and we'll process the
deletion request. See our [Privacy Policy](/PRIVACY_POLICY) for details.

**I want to disconnect StratosToDo from Claude**
Go to Claude's Settings → Connectors and remove StratosToDo. This doesn't
affect your account or data — you can reconnect any time.
